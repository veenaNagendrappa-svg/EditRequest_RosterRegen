Scenario matrix: the rows and rules the explorer refers to

Baseline in every example: Agency C, Mon/Wed, 4 weeks, 8 days, Duration 8. In the Combination explorer, the row matching your selection is highlighted.

Two rules that govern every row

  • Rule A. Pattern exists, agency changes, and dates or duration also change: Regenerate is always disabled. Agency-only (row 1) is the exception because it never touches date cycling.
  • Rule B. No pattern, any date-involving trigger: Regenerate is disabled only when the resulting day count is zero. Otherwise it is enabled, even if the agency also changed.

Also true across every row

  • The mapping (Rostered agency to New agency) stays visible when Regenerate is disabled. It is information, not a control.
  • With several agencies, the coordinator always confirms the mapping. Nothing is mapped automatically.
  • The Insufficient/Overallocated badge is always one combined total for the whole placement, never split per agency.
